# Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026

Gene Central Dogma Explorer is an educational app for exploring genes, transcripts,
sequences, and central-dogma concepts. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or
treatment guidance.

## Data the App Sends

When you use live lookup, the app sends the gene symbol, species name, and
optional transcript identifier to the Gene Central Dogma Explorer API. The API
uses Ensembl REST to retrieve gene and sequence information.

## Data the App Does Not Collect

The app does not use accounts, payments, ads, or analytics in version 1. The app
does not ask for your name, email address, health records, location, contacts,
photos, or other personal files.

## Saved Genes

Saved gene symbols are stored locally on your device so you can return to them
later. They are not synced to a server by version 1 of the app.

## Third-Party Services

Live gene lookup depends on Ensembl REST through the app backend. Your lookup
terms may be processed by the backend and Ensembl to return gene information.
